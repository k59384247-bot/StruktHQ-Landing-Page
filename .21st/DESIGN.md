# StruktHQ design direction

StruktHQ is a digital operations platform for organizations. Use the supplied wordmark and S-block logo, deep navy, blue, white and slate. Temporary approved typefaces are Fraunces and Manrope; final Recoleta and Axiforma require licensed webfonts. Keep body copy at 16px desktop / 14px mobile, headings at least 18px, and labels at 14px / 12px.

## October 18 product-led refresh

The user rejected repeated pastel rounded panels and floating icon tiles. This direction supersedes the earlier pastel-card refresh and frameless-image rule.

- Audiences: existing photography beside white copy, fine dividing rules and modest image corners. These are category illustrations, not customer evidence.
- Benefits: alternate copy and large product evidence. Use specific records from registrations, roles and executive profiles rather than shrinking the complete screen into a small panel.
- Election workflow: a numbered sequence with a shared horizontal rhythm on desktop and divided rows on narrower screens.
- Communication: a divided feature list beside the real message composer.
- Voting modes: two clear comparison columns, restrained surfaces, and existing numbered voter journeys.
- Product imagery: use unchanged native 3360px PNGs inline. CSS viewports remove unrelated navigation or focus on relevant records. Add restrained window chrome, contextual captions and a visible full-screen affordance. The viewer always opens the unchanged full upload. The participation monitor must retain every chart.
- Hover is subtle and limited to actionable screenshots. Maintain keyboard focus and disable movement for reduced motion.

## Reference findings

[Cal.com](https://cal.com) pairs concise copy with specific, readable product fragments inside quiet surfaces. Its feature visuals demonstrate the named task rather than displaying an entire dense screen at thumbnail size.

[TalentLMS](https://www.talentlms.com) gives product images substantial space beside the text, using context and small annotations to explain the value. Apply that spatial hierarchy using StruktHQ's real UI.

[Linear features](https://linear.app/features) uses a restrained visual hierarchy and product-specific compositions. The [Kage Linear gallery](https://kage.design/designs/linear-landing-page) was also reviewed as secondary design reference. Do not reproduce another site's branding or fabricate software interactions.

The 21st CLI search was attempted but the local account is not authenticated. Use the existing static stack; no additional component framework or dependency is needed.

Preserve approved product copy and avoid fabricated capability or customer claims.

Quality checks: desktop, tablet and phone layouts; all charts and intentional screenshot crops; image loading; accessible tabs and dialogs; visible focus; reduced motion; local build asset/link validation. Changes are for local review, without publishing.
