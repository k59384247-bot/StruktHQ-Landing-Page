# StruktHQ design direction

The cube logo connects the design to the idea of organizational structure. Deep navy and blue come from the supplied mark. White, pale ice blue, and slate provide readable supporting surfaces. The brand palette replaces TalentLMS green, preserving its generous product imagery, soft serif headings, rounded action buttons, and alternating light/dark rhythm.

Cal.com contributes a continuous centered frame, orderly section boundaries, aligned grids, and fine dividing lines. Use the exact supplied marketing copy as the content authority, excluding editorial comments and broken reference fragments.

Homepage: centered navy hero with layered real dashboard image; early metric proof; four audience cards; six-module interactive product showcase; three clear benefits; two distinct pricing entries; navy closing invitation; structured footer.

Elections: split navy hero with actual election management and participation screens; proof metrics; five-step workflow; restricted/public mode comparison; communication composer; COMPSSA case study; election audiences; voter-tier pricing; final CTA.

Requested typefaces are Recoleta and Axiforma. Exact files require webfont licensing. Layout work may continue while the user selects a preview font approach.

Quality checks: deliberate type hierarchy, readable product crops, varied section composition, 360–1440px responsiveness, keyboard and reduced-motion support, no fabricated product capabilities or social proof, local preview inspected before handoff.


## Approved photography and color refresh

Replace generic audience icons with consistent stock photography of students, professionals, community members, and educational institutions. These are category illustrations, not customer evidence. Use blue, teal, amber, and slate card treatments. Add a rich blue platform section, warm benefits surfaces, colored product frames, and stronger pricing hierarchy. Apply the same visual language to the Elections workflow, voting mode comparison, communication composer, and audience section. Preserve the content, Cal-style structure, native interaction, and inset modal chevrons.

Supporting copy: use a divided summary row for audience conclusions, a divider above the platform closing statement, and understated icon notes for election configuration and pricing clarifications. Text links use an underline and arrow with no box border; keyboard focus remains visible.


## Elections component refinements

Use the installed Untitled UI featured-icon variants and card spacing as references, adapted to the existing HTML/CSS build. Workflow steps are individual cards with gutters; tablet layouts use two columns and phones use one. Communication features use three restrained blue, teal, and amber card surfaces. The participation preview is contained within a complete, evenly inset browser frame. Enlarged screenshot views use unchanged original PNGs and offer a link to the full-resolution image.


## Approved capacity and hero refinements

Give each Elections voter tier a small teal people-icon tile beside its range, using stronger body type and a smaller voters caption. Prices retain their larger serif treatment. Repeat the existing cube watermark at the homepage hero’s top-right edge with lower opacity and smaller dimensions; crop it into the corner and keep headline/product content above it. Reduce the decorative mark on smaller screens.


## Frameless product images

Present screenshots without additional browser bars or colored image frames. Keep surrounding benefit cards and the monitoring section as content groups. Every product screenshot opens its original image using the same accessible button, with a subtle 1.8% zoom only for hover-capable pointers, visible focus, and no movement when reduced motion is requested. Audience photos and decorative brand marks do not imply clickability.
