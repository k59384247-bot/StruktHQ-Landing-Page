FROM SETUP TO RESULTS

Everything your Electoral Committee
needs to run an election.

  1. 01

    Configure

    Create your election, add positions and candidates, set the voting period and configure the rules.

  2. 02

    Set Up Voters

    Choose how voters will access the election — through a preloaded voter list or a public election link.

  3. 03

    Vote

    Voters verify their information, access their ballot, make their selections and review their choices before submitting.

  4. 04

    Count

    Votes are recorded and counted automatically as the election progresses.

  5. 05

    Report

    View election results, participation data and reports, and publish results immediately the election ends.

FLEXIBLE VOTING MODELS

Run your election your way.

Two ways to give your electorate access. One structured election workflow.

For defined electorates

Restricted Elections

Use this when your Electoral Committee already has a list of eligible voters.

  1. 01Upload your eligible voter list
  2. 02Configure voter information
  3. 03Voters verify their identity
  4. 04Eligible voters access their ballots
  5. 05Each voter can submit their vote once
  6. 06Results are automatically recorded and calculated
Best for

Student associations, departmental elections, faculty elections and organizations with a defined membership or electorate.

For open-access voting

Public Elections

Publish a dedicated election link that voters can access directly.

Voters provide the information required by the Electoral Committee through a registration form configured during election setup. Once registered, the system allows one vote per email address.

  1. 01Share election link
  2. 02Voter registers
  3. 03Access ballot
  4. 04Vote recorded
Best for

Elections where voters should be able to access the ballot without being preloaded into a voter list.

The Electoral Committee controls the information collected during registration and how the election is configured.

Every vote should count once.
