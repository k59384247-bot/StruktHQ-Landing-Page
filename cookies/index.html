Privacy Policy

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small values that a website can ask your browser to save. A cookie can help a site remember a choice between page visits. This notice covers the StruktHQ marketing website; an organization’s dashboard may have separate cookie information.

2. The cookie used on this website

Name: strukthq_cookie_choice. Provider: StruktHQ, as a first-party cookie on the website’s own domain. Purpose: remember your choice in the cookie controls so the notice does not reappear on every visit.

The cookie stores the value essential-only-v1. It does not store your name, email, organization, inquiry message or an individual tracking identifier. It expires after 180 days unless you clear it earlier. It applies across the pages of this website.

3. When the choice is saved

The cookie is saved when you choose “Use essential only” from the notice or Cookie settings. Browsing the site without making that choice does not create this cookie. Your choice does not enable analytics or advertising.

If your browser blocks cookies, the site remains usable, but your choice may not be remembered on a later visit. The cookie is set with SameSite=Lax and is marked Secure when the website is served over HTTPS.

Dismissing the notice with its close button hides it for the current page without saving a choice. It may appear again on another page or visit.

4. Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising cookies are used by this version of the website. There are no optional tracking services to switch on in Cookie settings.

If an optional service is added later, the cookie information and controls should be updated to identify it and provide any required choice before it is activated.

5. Manage or clear your choice

Open Cookie settings from the footer to see the current categories and your saved-choice status. Choose “Clear saved choice” to remove the website’s preference cookie. The notice will then be shown again.

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser’s settings. Clearing cookies or using a different browser or browser profile means your choice may need to be made again.

6. Questions and updates

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact admin@strukthq.com. The Privacy Policy explains the website’s inquiry process and broader information handling.

The date above identifies the latest draft. This notice should be reviewed when the website’s cookies or other storage technologies change.
